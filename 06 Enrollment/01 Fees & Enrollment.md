# Fees & Enrollment

## Launch Enrollment Flow
1. Parent submits Free Assessment form.
2. Inaya confirms assessment time.
3. Teacher conducts assessment.
4. Inaya recommends program and level.
5. Parent consultation explains roadmap and expectations.
6. Fees and schedule are confirmed.
7. Enrollment is recorded.
8. Student ID and profile are created.
9. Classes begin.

## Fees
Set the actual fee schedule separately before publishing. Do not advertise unapproved prices.

Recommended policy fields:
- Program
- Class frequency
- Class duration
- Monthly fee
- Payment due date
- Trial/assessment policy
- Make-up class policy
- Cancellation/refund policy
