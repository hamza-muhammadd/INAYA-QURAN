# FAQ

## Who is Inaya for?
Students who want structured Qur'an learning with clear assessment and progress monitoring.

## Which programs launch first?
Quran Reading, Tajweed & Recitation, and Hifz.

## How does placement work?
A free assessment is followed by a short live assessment and level recommendation.

## How do parents track progress?
Through teacher feedback, weekly review, monthly assessment, and progress reports.

## Can beginners join?
Yes. Beginners can start in Foundation/Quran Reading.

## Can an existing reader join?
Yes. The assessment determines the appropriate level.

## What if a student struggles?
The teacher identifies the specific weakness and creates a targeted intervention plan.

## Is Hifz available immediately?
Yes, for students whose assessment and readiness support structured memorization.

## How do I start?
Book a Free Quran Assessment.
