# Parent Guidelines

1. Help the student attend consistently and on time.
2. Provide a quiet place for class and practice.
3. Encourage daily practice without turning it into pressure.
4. Communicate schedule or learning concerns early.
5. Read teacher feedback and support the next target.
6. Do not compare the student with other students.
7. Respect teacher/student privacy.
8. Raise concerns directly and respectfully.
9. Understand that progress varies by age, starting level, attendance, practice, and individual needs.
10. Celebrate accuracy, consistency, effort, and improvement—not only speed.
