# Parent Consultation

## Simple Conversation

**What will my child learn?**
Show the Learning Roadmap and explain the student's program.

**Where will my child start?**
Explain that placement follows the free assessment.

**How will you know they are improving?**
Explain weekly progress review and monthly assessment.

**How will I know what happens in class?**
Explain teacher feedback and parent progress reporting.

**What if my child struggles?**
Explain the personalized intervention plan: identify the difficulty, adjust practice/lesson focus, monitor, and reassess.

**What happens after completing the level?**
Assessment → certificate/recognition where applicable → next level recommendation.
