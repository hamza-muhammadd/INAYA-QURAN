# Assessment Criteria

| Area | Observe |
|---|---|
| Reading | Letter recognition, joining, word accuracy |
| Makharij | Correct articulation of letters |
| Tajweed | Recognition and application of relevant rules |
| Fluency | Flow, hesitation, confidence |
| Memorization | Accuracy, retention, revision strength |

Assess the student at their actual level. Do not over-test simply to impress a parent.
