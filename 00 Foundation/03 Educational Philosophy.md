# Educational Philosophy

Inaya believes Qur'an should be taught with correctness, patience, structure, and care. Students should not simply complete lessons; they should develop accurate reading, sound Tajweed, confident recitation, disciplined memorization, and a lasting relationship with the Qur'an.

We assess before placement, teach according to level, correct gently and precisely, practice consistently, document progress, and move students forward only when they are ready.
