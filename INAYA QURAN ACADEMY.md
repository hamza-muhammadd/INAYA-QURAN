# 06 — HOW DOES A STUDENT ENTER INAYA?

A student does **not** simply choose a random class and start.

We want every student to begin at the level that is appropriate for them.

The basic journey is:

### STEP 01 — Registration

The student or parent submits the basic registration information.

We collect the information necessary to understand the student's learning needs.

---

### STEP 02 — Initial Assessment

The student's existing Qur’an level is assessed.

Depending on the program, the assessment may consider:

- Reading ability
- Pronunciation
- Makharij
- Basic Tajweed
- Fluency
- Memorisation
- Previous learning experience

The purpose is simple:

**To understand where the student is now.**

---

### STEP 03 — Level Placement

After assessment, the student is placed into the appropriate learning pathway.

A beginner should not be placed into an advanced class.

Likewise, an experienced student should not be forced to restart unnecessarily.

**Right student → Right level → Right program**

---

### STEP 04 — Learning Plan

The student's learning journey is structured according to their level and selected program.

This may include:

- Lessons
- Daily practice
- Homework
- Recitation practice
- Memorisation
- Revision
- Teacher feedback
- Progress monitoring

---

### STEP 05 — Regular Classes

The student begins structured classes with an assigned teacher.

The goal is not simply to complete lessons.

The goal is **measurable improvement**.

---

### STEP 06 — Practice & Feedback

Learning Qur’an requires consistent practice.

Students are therefore expected to practise outside class according to their learning plan.

Teachers provide feedback and guide students toward correction.

---

### STEP 07 — Progress Tracking

The student's progress is monitored throughout the learning journey.

The long-term Inaya learning system is designed to include:

- Attendance
- Daily lessons
- Homework
- Practice recording
- Teacher feedback
- Progress analytics
- Parent dashboard
- Achievement system
- Certificates

---

### STEP 08 — Assessment

Students are periodically assessed to determine whether they have achieved the required learning outcomes.

This allows the Academy to identify:

**What has been learned → What needs improvement → What comes next**

---

### STEP 09 — Progression

When a student successfully meets the requirements of their current level, they progress to the next stage.

Therefore, Inaya is intended to function as a **learning pathway**, not a collection of disconnected classes.
